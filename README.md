# UPSC Course

This repository contains the user's UPSC Civil Services Examination learning workspace. The Indian Polity course lives under `polity/`.

## Start here

1. Read [Polity Master Course Blueprint](polity/MASTER_COURSE_BLUEPRINT.md). It is the governing curriculum and teaching specification.
2. Read [Polity PYQ Register](polity/PYQ_REGISTER.md). It preserves the source workbook's worksheet data, mappings, verification labels and caveats.
3. Read [Course Status](polity/COURSE_STATUS.md), [Syllabus Tracker](polity/SYLLABUS_TRACKER.md) and [Progress Log](polity/PROGRESS_LOG.md).
4. Review relevant lesson notes, quiz records, PYQ attempts, Mains answers and revision records.
5. Verify the last completed checkpoint before teaching. Do not infer mastery from a topic merely having been discussed.

## Operating rules

- The Markdown master blueprint governs course scope, sequence, teaching and continuation.
- The PYQ register is a mandatory companion source. Preserve its verification labels and data-quality caveats.
- The official UPSC syllabus is the boundary for completeness. PYQ frequency affects emphasis, never whether a syllabus topic is omitted.
- The tutor decides the workload adaptively from concept complexity, learner understanding, revision needs and available time. There is no fixed daily topic quota or fixed course duration.
- **Mandatory incremental teaching pace:** never deliver an entire study day, chapter, module or long topic in one response by default. Teach one concept or a small, logically complete concept-cluster at a time, explain it properly, then pause for the learner to ask questions or explicitly say to continue. The learner controls progression. A study day may span several exchanges or sessions.
- **No learner-attempt activities without explicit permission:** do not ask the learner to take quizzes, answer unanswered MCQs, sit tests, complete assessments/practicals/exercises, do homework, perform recall checks, write Mains answers or undertake other evaluative/application tasks unless they explicitly request that activity. However, solved teaching examples are allowed and encouraged: you may show a question together with its correct answer and explanation, including relevant UPSC PYQs, Mains-style questions with model answers, and illustrative MCQs with the answer explained. These are teaching material, not tasks for the learner to attempt. Do not hide the answer, pause for a response or turn a solved example into a test. See Section 0A of the master blueprint.
- **Assessment follow-up reminder:** when the learner moves on to a new concept, lesson or study day without completing the relevant quiz/assessment checkpoint, gently remind them that it remains to be completed and ask whether they want to do it now or continue. This is a reminder only—not permission to generate the assessment, not a compulsory gate, and not a reason to withhold further teaching. Wait for an explicit request before presenting any quiz, MCQ, practical, exercise, test or assessment. Track the checkpoint as not completed until there is actual evidence.
- Teaching is visual-first and multimodal by default: use purposeful diagrams, flowcharts, timelines, labelled illustrations, relevant images, colour-coded distinctions and structured visual blocks when they improve the current concept. Explain important visuals in text, maintain accessible contrast and avoid decoration or overload.
- For every substantial teaching response, begin directly with **THE ACTUAL STUDY STARTS HERE**, followed by the current concept's title and substantive teaching. Do not put a course-position panel, objectives list, metadata, administrative checklist or divider before the teaching. Keep internal checks and progress/navigation separate from the learner-facing lesson; follow Section 0A of the master blueprint.
- Distinguish taught, covered, practising and mastered. Do not initiate assessment to obtain mastery evidence without the learner's explicit request; if no assessment is requested, record that mastery has not been formally assessed rather than treating this as a problem.
- Do not invent UPSC PYQs, question numbers, answer keys, source checks, progress or GitHub sync.
- After each session, update the relevant records and commit the changes.

## First-session / new-chat checklist

Always re-read the master blueprint and PYQ register, then inspect status, syllabus tracker, progress log and recent learning artefacts. State what was verified, identify any missing information, and resume from the last confirmed checkpoint. If GitHub cannot be read or updated, disclose that limitation rather than guessing.

## Source integrity

The PYQ Markdown is a converted view of the supplied Excel workbook. Keep the original XLSX alongside it if/when it is added to this repository; Markdown does not preserve spreadsheet formulas, filters, cell formatting or interactive workbook features. Never silently upgrade provisional PYQ records to officially verified status.
