# Lesson Notes

Store one Markdown file per lesson or closely related topic. Suggested filename: `YYYY-MM-DD-module-XX-topic-slug.md`.

## Learner-facing lesson format

Do not display a separate pre-lesson brief, course-position panel, learning-objectives section, source-verification table or administrative checklist before the lesson. Do not make the learner scroll past or dismiss a preamble to reach the teaching.

Start the visible lesson directly with this heading, followed immediately by the lesson title and teaching:

```markdown
# THE ACTUAL STUDY STARTS HERE

# [Lesson title]

[Begin teaching immediately.]
```

The heading must be the first visible lesson content. No divider, Part A, metadata block or preliminary checklist is needed before it.

## Tutor/repository preparation (not displayed as a pre-lesson section)

Before teaching, the tutor must still check and maintain the necessary information internally and in the appropriate repository records:
- Course position and last verified checkpoint.
- Learning objectives and UPSC syllabus connection.
- Scope, boundaries and prerequisites.
- Constitutional/statutory sources and verification status.
- PYQ mappings, exact identifiers and original verification labels/caveats.
- Assessment plan and any learner-specific gaps.

These checks are for accuracy, planning and progress management. They must not be rendered as a separate visible pre-lesson section. Include source notes or citations naturally at the relevant point in the lesson when they help the learner understand or verify a claim.

## Lesson content

After the opening heading and lesson title, teach from first principles. Include clear explanations, constitutional/statutory references, examples, judgments where relevant, meaningful diagrams/tables, Prelims distinctions and traps, Mains dimensions and answer-writing application.

Conclude with a concise recap and, where appropriate, learner practice. Record learner responses, demonstrated understanding, unresolved doubts, covered-but-not-mastered areas and revision action in repository progress records—not as a compulsory administrative block at the top of the lesson.

Label tutor-created questions as original practice, never as UPSC PYQs. Teaching or reading alone does not establish mastery.
