# Lesson Notes

Store one Markdown file per lesson or closely related topic. Suggested filename: `YYYY-MM-DD-module-XX-topic-slug.md`.

## Mandatory document structure

Every substantial lesson note must have two clearly separated parts. Planning, administrative information and verification must appear before the lesson. Actual teaching must begin only after the dedicated content-start heading.

### Part A — Learning brief and verification (before the lesson)

Include, as applicable:

- Course position: module, topic sequence, last verified checkpoint, current topic and next topic. Label uncertain progress as unverified.
- Learning objectives: specific abilities the learner should demonstrate by the end.
- Syllabus connection: relevant UPSC Prelims GS-I and Mains GS-II syllabus clauses.
- Scope and boundaries: what this lesson covers and what is deferred or cross-referenced.
- Prerequisites: prior concepts needed; identify any prerequisite gap.
- Sources and verification: constitutional Articles, Schedules, Acts, rules, official documents, judgments and textbook references. Distinguish primary/official sources from secondary explanations.
- PYQ relevance: mapped questions, exact identifiers and the PYQ Register's verification labels/caveats. Do not call a question verified unless the register supports it.
- Assessment plan: intended recall, MCQs, actual PYQs or Mains application.

Keep this section concise and factual. Do not begin explaining the subject here.

### Mandatory boundary

After Part A, insert a horizontal divider and this exact heading:

# LESSON / STUDY STARTS HERE

The substantive lesson title and all teaching must appear below this heading. Nothing that teaches the topic—including an introductory explanation, worked example, diagram explaining the subject or substantive topic heading—may appear above it.

### Part B — Lesson / study content (after the boundary)

Begin with the lesson title, then teach from first principles. Include clear explanations, constitutional/statutory references, examples, judgments where relevant, meaningful diagrams/tables, Prelims distinctions and traps, Mains dimensions and answer-writing application.

End with a recap, learner recall/application evidence, unresolved doubts, covered-but-not-mastered areas and revision action.

Label tutor-created questions as original practice, never as UPSC PYQs. Record what the learner actually demonstrated; teaching or reading alone does not establish mastery.
