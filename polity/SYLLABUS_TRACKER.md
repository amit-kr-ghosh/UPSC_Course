## Status definitions
- Not started: no lesson recorded.
- Introduced: explained, understanding not established.
- Practising: applying through MCQs, PYQs or answer writing.
- Covered: planned teaching completed; mastery not established.
- Mastered / Demonstrated: understanding demonstrated through assessment.
- Revision due: revisit through recall or assessment.
- Deferred: postponed intentionally; record reason.

## Update rules
1. Update only after an actual lesson or assessment.
2. Record exact topic, date, evidence and next action.
3. Do not mark a module complete while required topics remain uncovered.
4. Do not mark mastery without independent recall/application evidence.
5. Reconcile with lesson notes and progress log every session.
